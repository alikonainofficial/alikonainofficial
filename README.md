### ⚙️ What I Do

I build practical software systems that turn complex business requirements into reliable products, tools, and workflows.

My work spans:

* **Full-stack product engineering** with Next.js, React, NestJS, Node.js, Python, and Java
* **Internal platforms and dashboards** for operations, content, growth, and product teams
* **Data ingestion and automation pipelines** for metadata, media, APIs, and large-scale workflows
* **Recommendation, categorization, and enrichment systems** using Python and AI-assisted tooling
* **AI-powered workflow automation** for creative systems, media generation, content operations, and growth tooling
* **Production engineering** across APIs, databases, QA, CI/CD, Docker, cloud workflows, and reliability improvements

Most of my work sits at the intersection of:

### 🧩 Engineering Approach

* Build systems, not one-off scripts
* Optimize for repeatability, reliability, and scale
* Automate manual workflows when the business value is clear
* Treat AI as a useful system component, not a gimmick
* Prefer practical software that works over impressive demos that break
* Keep improving the pipeline until it becomes boring, predictable, and useful
